OPERATION GHOSTPRINT

OSINT FIELD EXERCISE · 25–30 MINUTES

Situation report

A fictional contractor, Asterion Dynamics, has identified public posts referring to a potential disruption of its Helios demonstration. An unidentified persona known as NullSpecter may be connected to a developer account and planning notes.

Your mission is to assess the public evidence — not to pursue or contact a person.

Mission objectives

  1. Find the secondary handle linked to NullSpecter.
  2. Identify the project codename and associated fictional host.
  3. Find the relevant date and determine whether the planned action is confirmed or only alleged.
  4. Spot the false attribution lead.
  5. Submit an assessment with evidence URLs and confidence.

Rules of engagement

Passive public research only. Do not contact real people, send messages, attempt logins, scan hosts or collect personal information. All people, organizations and incident claims in this simulation are fictional. Real third-party websites are research references only.
